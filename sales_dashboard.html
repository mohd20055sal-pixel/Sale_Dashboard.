📈 Growth Trajectory

Revenue grew consistently from $479K in 2015 to $722K in 2018 — a 50.7% increase. Technology led growth in 2017–2018 with Office Supplies catching up in 2018.

🌍 Geographic Concentration

California alone accounts for 19.7% of all revenue ($446K). Top 3 states (CA, NY, TX) contribute over 40% — high geographic risk concentration.

👤 Consumer Dominance

The Consumer segment drives 50.8% of revenue at $1.15M. Corporate and Home Office segments are underserved relative to their potential purchase power.

📦 Shipping Inefficiency

59.3% of revenue ships via Standard Class. Same Day delivery represents only 5.5% — an opportunity to upsell premium shipping to high-value orders.

⭐ Star Products

Canon imageCLASS 2200 copier alone generated $61.6K — 2.7% of total revenue from a single SKU. Technology hardware drives disproportionate value.

🔁 Seasonality Signal

Q3 (Sep) and Q4 (Nov–Dec) consistently peak. September 2018 hit $86K, November 2018 peaked at $118K — plan inventory and campaigns accordingly.
